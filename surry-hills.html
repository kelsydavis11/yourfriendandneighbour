Keys & door

Please don't use the top deadlock. It can become a little sticky once locked and isn't required.

Just use the regular lower door-handle lock when locking the apartment.

📺

TV & Chromecast

The TV has Chromecast so you can cast from streaming apps on your phone once you're connected to the apartment WiFi.

The power button on the remote can be sticky. If it doesn't respond, use the physical power button behind the TV on the back left. The other remote buttons should work normally.

🏊

Gym, pool & sauna

The gym, pool and sauna are open from 5am daily.

They're on the ground floor, to the right of the lifts as you approach.

🧺

Our local favourites

Burger · Downstairs

Quick & Dirty

The famous one, just downstairs on the corner of Elizabeth and Reservoir. Incredible smash-patty burger and it's only $10 — unheard of. No discount needed. Perfect chips too.

Coffee · Reservoir St

Single O

Our pick for the best coffee nearby. Famous, usually a huge line, and the hype is real if you're a coffee addict.

Wine bar · Date night

Bar Copains

Incredible wine bar with an amazing wine and tapas menu. Perfect for afternoon drinks or date night. Book if you want to avoid lining up, but it's worth it. Everything is delicious — our favourites are the bacon & Comté tart and the whiting sandwich.

Indian fusion · Any occasion

Don't Tell Aunty

⚡ Check EatClub

One of our all-time favourites — we go every few weeks. Great for dates, groups, a long lunch or a quick dinner, with fun interiors, excellent cocktails and consistently top-tier Indian food. We'd go regardless of the discount.

Cocktails · Neighbourhood bar

Rover

⚡ Often on EatClub

A great neighbourhood cocktail bar with a killer martini and a very good burger. If you see it on EatClub, snag it.

Pub · Crown St

The Dolphin

A stylish upmarket pub with a seriously good Italian kitchen. It does everything: proper sit-down dinner in the dining room, casual pizzas in the bar and a great all-round option for almost any occasion.

Middle Eastern · Crown St

Nour

⚡ Check EatClub

A huge score if you find it on EatClub. Outstanding modern Middle Eastern food, beautiful interiors and a great proper dinner destination.

Thai · Foveaux St

Chuuan Chim

⚡ Often on EatClub

Casual, consistently good Thai for a quick lunch or takeaway. Another huge-value EatClub find when it pops up.
