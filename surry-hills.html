House manual

The things most worth knowing.

Short, practical instructions for the apartment. If anything is unclear, message us and we'll help.

🔑

Keys & door

Please don't use the top deadlock. It can become a little sticky once locked and isn't required.

Just use the regular lower door-handle lock when locking the apartment.

📺

TV & Chromecast

The TV has Chromecast so you can cast from streaming apps on your phone once you're connected to the apartment WiFi.

The power button on the remote can be sticky. If it doesn't respond, use the physical power button behind the TV on the back left. The other remote buttons should work normally.

🏊

Gym, pool & sauna

The gym, pool and sauna are open from 5am daily.

They're on the ground floor, to the right of the lifts as you approach.

🧺

Laundry

There is a washing machine in the apartment and a drying rack on the balcony.

🍳

Kitchen

To light the gas stovetop, turn the relevant gas knob and hold down the black igniter button until the burner lights.

Perks & local favourites

The places we'd actually send friends staying here — from a $10 burger downstairs to some of our favourite date-night spots in Sydney.

⚡Always check EatClub around here.

Surry Hills is stacked with genuinely great venues on EatClub. These are our favourites regardless of whether they're discounted — the tags just flag the ones worth checking in the app before you head out.
I promise I don't work for them — just a huge fan 😂

🍔 Burger · Downstairs

The Famous

Just downstairs on the corner of Elizabeth and Reservoir. Incredible smash-patty burger and it's only $10 — genuinely unheard of. No discount needed. Perfect chips too.

🥐 Bakery · Coffee

AP Bakery

Insane pastries and baked goods, plus great coffee. There are a few locations now, but the flagship on Paramount Street is nearby and an absolute must-go.

🍷 Wine bar · Date night

Bar Copains

Incredible wine bar with an amazing wine and tapas menu. Perfect for afternoon drinks or date night. Book if you want to avoid lining up, but it's worth it. Everything is delicious — our favourites are the bacon & Comté tart and the whiting sandwich.

☕ Coffee · Reservoir St

Single O

Famous for a reason. Usually a huge line, and the hype is real if you're a coffee addict. Right around the corner on Reservoir Street.

🍛 Indian fusion · Any occasion

Don't Tell Aunty

⚡ EatClub

One of our all-time favourites — we go every few weeks. Great for dates, groups, a long lunch or a quick dinner, with fun interiors, excellent cocktails and consistently top-tier Indian food.

🍸 Cocktails · Neighbourhood bar

Rover

⚡ EatClub

A great neighbourhood cocktail bar with a killer martini and a very good burger.

🍕 Pub · Crown St

The Dolphin

A stylish upmarket pub with a seriously good Italian kitchen. It does everything: proper sit-down dinner in the dining room, casual pizzas in the bar and a great all-round option for almost any occasion.

✨ Middle Eastern · Crown St

Nour

⚡ EatClub

Outstanding modern Middle Eastern food, beautiful interiors and a great proper dinner destination.

🍜 Thai · Foveaux St

Chuuan Chim

⚡ EatClub

Casual, consistently good Thai for a quick lunch or takeaway.
