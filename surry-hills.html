House manual

The things most worth knowing.

Short, practical instructions for the apartment. If anything is unclear, message us and we'll help.

🔑

Keys & door

Please don't use the top deadlock. It can become a little sticky once locked and isn't required.

Just use the regular lower door-handle lock when locking the apartment.

📺

TV & Chromecast

The TV has Chromecast so you can cast from streaming apps on your phone once you're connected to the apartment WiFi.

The power button on the remote can be sticky. If it doesn't respond, use the physical power button behind the TV on the back left. The other remote buttons should work normally.

🏊

Gym, pool & sauna

The gym, pool and sauna are open from 5am daily.

They're on the ground floor, to the right of the lifts as you approach.

🧺

Laundry

There is a washing machine in the apartment and a drying rack on the balcony.

🍳

Kitchen

To light the gas stovetop, turn the relevant gas knob and hold down the black igniter button until the burner lights.
