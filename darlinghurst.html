House manual & FAQ

The practical stuff.

The things guests most often need while they're here. We'll keep adding to this as we build out the full guide.

TV & Chromecast

Turn the living room TV on using the black universal remote. The TV has Chromecast, so you can cast streaming services from your phone.

If the power button on the remote isn't working, turn the TV on using the button on the back of the TV at the bottom right. It is also worth checking that the TV is switched on at the wall.

Laundry

The washing machine works well. There is also a clothes drying rack in the wardrobe.

Please note: the tumble dryer does not work, so please don't try to use it.

Car park & bins

To take the lift down to the car park and bins, turn the small key in the elevator and then press G.
