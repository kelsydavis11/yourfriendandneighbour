The things most worth knowing.

Short, practical instructions for the apartment. If anything is unclear, message us and we'll help.

TV & Chromecast

The TV is Chromecast-only. The apartment does not have an aerial for free-to-air TV, but you can stream from almost any app on your phone — including live TV through the free-to-air apps.

Make sure your phone is connected to WiFi-DBD, turn the TV on using the physical power button behind the TV on the left side, then make sure the source is set to HDMI 3. Open your streaming app, tap the Chromecast icon and select the TV that appears.

The remote power button is sticky, but the volume and other remote buttons work normally.

💡

Living room lamp

Turn the lamp on and off using the wall switch only. The switch on the lamp itself is taped because it does not work.

🍳

Stove & oven

Before using either the stovetop or oven, make sure the middle switch on the power outlet on the left kitchen splashback, labelled OVEN, is switched on.

The stovetop is touch-activated induction. Press and hold the power button to switch it on.

🚗

Car park & bin room access

In the lift, use the small key and hold it turned while pressing G for the ground floor.

The bin rooms are on the right as you come out of the lift.

🧺

Laundry

The washing machine works normally and there is a clothes drying rack in the wardrobe.

Please note: the tumble dryer does not work and is not listed as an amenity.
