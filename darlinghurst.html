TV not turning on?

TV & Chromecast

Turn the living room TV on using the black universal remote. The TV has Chromecast, so you can cast streaming services from your phone.

If the remote power button isn't working: turn the TV on using the button on the back of the TV at the bottom right, and make sure the TV is switched on at the wall.

Laundry

Washing & drying

The washing machine works well. There is a clothes drying rack in the wardrobe.

Please note: the tumble dryer does not work and is not listed as an amenity.

Building access

Car park & bins

To take the lift down to the car park and bins, turn the small key in the elevator and then press G.
